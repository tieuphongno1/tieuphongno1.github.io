Flow MCN

Flow MCN is an internal desktop tool for our multi-channel network. Staff track YouTube channels they manage: views, estimated revenue, and reports. It is not a public consumer app and is not offered in any store.

YouTube access

A channel owner (or the Google account that owns the brand channel) signs in with Google once. Flow MCN then reads channel metadata, Analytics, and estimated revenue for dashboards used by our team. We do not post, delete, or change YouTube content.

Who uses it

  • Our MCN operators and managers, via the Flow MCN desktop app
  • Channel owners only to grant read-only YouTube access
