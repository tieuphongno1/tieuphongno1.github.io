Flow MCN Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · Home

Flow MCN is a desktop application operated by our company for our multi-channel network. YouTube channel owners use their own Google accounts, including Gmail addresses outside our company, to grant read-only access. Staff then view that data in the desktop app. This policy explains what we collect when someone connects a YouTube channel, how we use it, and how to revoke access. Flow MCN is not a public consumer app and is not offered in any store.

1. Data we receive from Google / YouTube

When a channel owner authorizes Flow MCN, we request read-only OAuth scopes and may store:

  • YouTube channel ID, title, and thumbnail
  • Public and Analytics metrics (views, watch time, subscribers, and similar)
  • Estimated revenue and related monetary Analytics (if the account is the brand owner and YouTube provides them)
  • An encrypted OAuth refresh token so we can refresh data without asking the owner to sign in every day

We do not request scopes to upload, edit, comment, or delete YouTube videos.

2. Other data in the app

Staff accounts (name, role, login) are created by our admins. Login uses our own username/password, not Google Sign-In for staff. Channel assignment and revenue-share settings are entered by our team.

3. How we use the data

  • Show dashboards, tracking, and reports to authorized staff
  • Sync Analytics periodically so numbers stay current
  • Calculate net revenue using our configured share percent

We do not sell YouTube data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with third parties except infrastructure we already use to run the app (database and hosting under our control).

4. Storage and security

Data is stored in our production database. OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM). Access inside Flow MCN is limited by role (admin / manager / staff).

5. Retention

Channel metrics and tokens stay while the channel remains linked for our operations. If a channel is unlinked or a staff account is removed, we stop new syncs and may delete or anonymize records according to our company process.

6. How to revoke YouTube access

The channel owner can revoke Flow MCN at any time in their Google Account:

https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

After revoke, our refresh token stops working and we cannot pull new Analytics.

8. Chính sách (tiếng Việt)

Flow MCN là phần mềm desktop cho mạng lưới kênh. Chủ kênh YouTube dùng tài khoản Google của chính họ (gồm địa chỉ Gmail ngoài công ty) để cấp quyền chỉ đọc. Nhân sự xem lượt xem, doanh thu ước tính và báo cáo trong ứng dụng desktop. Flow MCN không phải ứng dụng tiêu dùng công khai và không có trên bất kỳ cửa hàng nào. Chúng tôi chỉ đọc thông tin kênh, lượt xem, và doanh thu ước tính — không đăng/sửa/xóa video, không bán dữ liệu. Token đăng nhập YouTube được mã hóa. Chủ kênh gỡ quyền bất kỳ lúc nào tại trang quyền Google ở trên.
